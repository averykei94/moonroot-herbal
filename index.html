THE PERSON BEHIND MOONROOT

About the Founder

Indigo

Founder, Moonroot Apothecary · PhD in Bioengineering

My background is in bioengineering: I hold a PhD, and my doctoral research focused on infection control, including work with pre- and probiotic interventions. That's part of why gut health sits at the center of so much of what we grow here. It's not just a wellness trend to me, it's where the science already pointed.

What pulled me toward herbalism was the gap between wellness and modern medicine. There's a long history of herbal traditions now being validated by real research, but there's also a wellness industry generating billions in sales on products that, unlike pharmaceuticals, aren't required to prove their safety or effectiveness to the FDA before reaching the shelf. People deserve to know what they're putting in their bodies. They want community, trust, and autonomy in their own health, so I wanted to build something that brings both traditional plant knowledge and scientific rigor to the table, giving people practices they can actually trust.

Moonroot has grown slower than I first planned. I started building it while adjusting to motherhood and a new job, and I wanted to be intentional rather than rush it. It's coming back now, rooted here in North Texas, herb by herb.
